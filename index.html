System Architecture & Workflows

Built with self-hosted n8n, Groq LLM (llama-3.1-8b-instant), Meta Graph API, Google Sheets, and Telegram.

1. Fenjan DM Orders (21 Nodes)

Listens to Messenger & Instagram DMs, filters echoes, parses intent & items via Groq AI, calculates exact prices in JS, saves carts/orders to Google Sheets, and alerts the kitchen.

⬇ Download Workflow JSON

2. Daily Post & Kitchen Progress

Publishes a daily menu highlight at 9:00 AM Cairo time, and serves an n8n Form Trigger so kitchen staff can update order statuses and notify customers automatically.

📊 View Menu Structure

3. Fenjan Error Handler

Catches any execution or Meta delivery failure across the system, logs the stack trace and execution URL to the Errors sheet tab, and sends an instant Telegram alert.
